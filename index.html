How I can help

Specialist input when a patient is critically ill

Critical care medicine supports patients whose heart, lungs, kidneys or other organs are failing and need close monitoring and active treatment in an intensive care unit.

Advanced ICU care

Management of critically ill patients who need continuous monitoring, ventilator support, control of severe infection, and coordinated care across specialties.

ECMO & life support

Involvement in selected cases of severe heart or lung failure where extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO) may be considered by the treating team as a bridge to recovery.

Second-opinion pathway

A clear route for families, referring doctors and departments seeking specialist input on complex ICU questions, ventilation strategy or ECMO candidacy.

About

A clinician grounded in training and research

Dr. Oendrila Roy trained in anaesthesiology before completing super-specialty training (DrNB) in Critical Care Medicine. She now practises as a Consultant in Critical Care Medicine at Apollo Multispeciality Hospitals, Kolkata, with a particular focus on ECMO, and has authored peer-reviewed work on ECMO and mechanical ventilation.
