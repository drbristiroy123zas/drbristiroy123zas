About

Dr. Oendrila Roy

A critical care physician whose practice sits at the intersection of intensive care medicine, extracorporeal life support and academic research.

Dr. Oendrila Roy is a Consultant in Critical Care Medicine and ECMO Services at Apollo Multispeciality Hospitals, Kolkata.

Her clinical work centres on patients who are critically ill — those who need continuous monitoring, breathing support, and treatment for failing organs in an intensive care unit. Day to day, this means managing severe respiratory failure and ARDS, sepsis and septic shock, and complications that affect several organ systems at once.

She trained first in anaesthesiology, which builds a detailed understanding of the airway, breathing, circulation and the drugs used to support them. She then completed super-specialty training (DrNB) in Critical Care Medicine, focused entirely on the care of the sickest patients in the hospital. Within critical care, she has developed a particular interest in extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO) — a form of life support used, in carefully selected cases, when the heart or lungs cannot do enough on their own.

Alongside clinical practice she contributes to peer-reviewed research and teaching, including a textbook chapter on ECMO and published case work on extracorporeal support and mechanical ventilation.

Critical care is a team endeavour — families, nurses, therapists and consultants working from the same information toward the same goal.

Academic work

Research, writing and teaching

Dr. Roy's published work reflects her clinical interests: extracorporeal support for severe heart and lung failure, and the practical details of mechanical ventilation in the ICU. She has contributed a chapter on ECMO to the ISCCM Textbook of Critical Care Medicine and co-authored peer-reviewed case reports and a case series on extracorporeal membrane oxygenation.
